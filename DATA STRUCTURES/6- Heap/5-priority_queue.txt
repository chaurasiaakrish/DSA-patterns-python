In priority queue elements are removed according to their priority, not their insertion order.
Priority Queue (minimum priority value first)
10
2
7
First out: 2
Even though 10 was inserted first, 2 comes out first because it has the highest priority in this example.


A heap is a data structure that efficiently maintains the element with the highest priority 
(for a min heap, the smallest element).

        2
       / \
      5   3
     / \
    8   7

The smallest value, 2, is at the root. When we remove it, the heap rearranges itself to maintain its property.
A heap is one of the most common ways to implement a priority queue efficiently.

A priority queue is the abstract data type, while a heap is a data structure used to implement it. 
So they behave correspondingly, but they aren't technically the same thing.
