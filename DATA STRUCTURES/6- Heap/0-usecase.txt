When should you recognize a heap problem?

Look for questions asking for:

- The Kth largest or smallest element.
- The top K frequent elements.
- Repeatedly retrieving the minimum or maximum.
- Merging K sorted lists.
- Scheduling tasks by priority.
- Finding the median from a continuous stream of numbers.
- Processing the next event with the earliest time.

A heap is particularly useful when you need to repeatedly retrieve the smallest or largest item without sorting all the data every time.
