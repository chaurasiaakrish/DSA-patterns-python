1. Sliding Window

[       window       ]
 ↑                   ↑
left                right

You move left and right around to find something like:

longest window satisfying a condition
shortest window satisfying a condition
window with at most K...
etc.
The important thing is that the window can be expanded/shrunk intelligently.

2. Kadane's Algorithm 

What is the maximum-sum contiguous subarray?
[-2, 3, -1, 4, -5]
Kadane finds:
[3, -1, 4]
because its sum is 6.

You don't need prefix sum to solve that.

3. Prefix sum

It gives you a way to represent cumulative information so that differences between two positions give you a subarray sum.
Prefix at R - Prefix before L = Sum from L to R
